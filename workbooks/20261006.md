## テーマ: The Joy of Cooking at Home (家で料理をする楽しさ)

### 英文パッセージ

Cooking is a great hobby. Many people like to cook at home every day. Why is cooking at home so good? There are many reasons.

First, cooking at home is healthy. When you cook, you know what is in your food. You can use many fresh vegetables and good meat. You can also use less salt and oil. This is good for your body.

Second, you can save money. Eating at a restaurant is expensive. Buying food at a supermarket is cheaper. You can make many meals with a little money.

Third, you can make your favorite taste. Do you like spicy food? You can add a lot of pepper. Do you like sweet food? You can add some honey. You can choose your favorite ingredients.

Also, cooking is a way to learn new things. You can try new recipes from books or the internet. Sometimes you fail, but that is okay. You can learn from mistakes and become a better cook.

Finally, cooking makes people happy. You can cook for your family or your friends. When they eat your food and say, "This is delicious!", you feel very happy. It is a wonderful feeling to share food with people you love.

You do not need to be a professional. Start with something simple, like a salad or an egg. Let’s enjoy cooking!
