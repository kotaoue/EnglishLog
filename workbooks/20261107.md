## テーマ: A Morning Walk (朝の散歩)

### 英文パッセージ

I wake up at seven o'clock every morning. The sun is very bright today. I am happy to see the blue sky. I put on my favorite shoes. I go outside for a walk in my neighborhood. The air is fresh and the weather is cool.

I see many things during my walk. First, I go to a small park. The park has green grass. There are big trees and beautiful flowers. The flowers are red, yellow, and blue. I see a small dog in the park. The dog is white and very cute. It runs fast to catch a ball. Its owner is a kind girl.

Next, I see a cat. The cat is black and white. It is sleeping quietly under a big tree. It looks very peaceful. I also see many birds in the trees. The birds are singing. Their voices are very sweet.

Then, I see my friend, Ken. Ken is running near the park. He wears a blue T-shirt. He sees me and smiles. He says, "Good morning!" I say, "Good morning, Ken! How are you?" He says, "I am fine, thank you." We talk for a short time.

I walk for thirty minutes. Then I go back to my house. I am a little hungry now. I drink a glass of cold water. I eat a piece of bread and a red apple for breakfast. It is a very good start to the day. I love my morning walk. It makes me feel strong and healthy.
