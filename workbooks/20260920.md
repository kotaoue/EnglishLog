## テーマ: The Importance of Breakfast (朝食の重要性)

### 英文パッセージ

Many people are busy in the morning. They wake up late, get dressed quickly, and leave their homes without eating anything. However, health experts say that breakfast is the most important meal of the day. Eating breakfast gives your body and brain the energy they need to start the day correctly.

First, breakfast helps you focus. When you sleep at night, your body uses its energy. If you do not eat in the morning, your blood sugar level stays low. This makes it difficult to think clearly at school or work. According to some studies, students who eat breakfast usually get better grades than those who skip it.

Second, eating breakfast can help you stay healthy. Some people think that skipping breakfast helps them lose weight. Actually, the opposite is often true. If you skip breakfast, you will feel very hungry later and might eat too much lunch or choose unhealthy snacks. A balanced breakfast, such as eggs, fruit, and yogurt, keeps you full for a long time and provides good vitamins.

In conclusion, even if you are busy, you should try to eat something in the morning. A small meal, like a piece of fruit or a glass of milk, is much better than nothing. It will help you stay active and healthy throughout the day. Start your day with a good breakfast to make your life better.
