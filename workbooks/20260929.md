## テーマ: The Joy of Having a Pet (ペットを飼う喜び)

### 英文パッセージ

Many people around the world like to have pets in their homes. The most popular pets are dogs and cats, but some people like birds or fish too. Having a pet can change your life in many good ways. 

First, a pet is a great friend. For example, when you come home from school or work, a dog greets you at the door. They are always happy to see you. You can go for a walk with your dog in the park every morning or evening. This is a very good way to get exercise and stay healthy.

Second, pets can help you relax. Cats are often very quiet and like to sleep. Many people feel calm when they sit on a sofa with a cat. Watching fish swim in water is also very relaxing for many people. It helps them forget about their busy day.

However, having a pet is a big responsibility. You must take care of them every day. This means you need to give them healthy food and fresh water. You also need to clean their living space. If your pet gets sick, you must take them to a doctor for animals, called a veterinarian.

In conclusion, pets are like family members. They stay with you and give you love. They listen to you when you are sad, and they play with you when you are happy. Many studies show that people with pets feel less stress and more happiness. If you have enough time and love to give, having a pet is a wonderful experience.
