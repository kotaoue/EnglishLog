## テーマ: A Trip to the Supermarket（スーパーマーケットへのお買い物）

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Saturday. My name is Ken. I go to the supermarket near my house. The supermarket is very big and clean. I see many kinds of food there.

First, I go to the fruit section. I want to buy apples. I see red apples and green apples. I like red apples because they are sweet. I put four red apples in my shopping bag. 

Next, I look for milk. I drink milk every morning for breakfast. The milk is near the eggs and butter. I take one white bottle of milk. 

Then, I go to the bread area. The bread smells very good. It is fresh. I buy one loaf of white bread for my sandwiches. 

Now, I have apples, milk, and bread. I am a little hungry. I see some chocolate near the register. I like chocolate very much. I buy one small chocolate bar too. 

I go to the cashier to pay. The cashier is a very kind woman. She smiles and says, "Hello." I smile and say, "Hello" too. I pay for the food with my money. I put all my food in a brown paper bag. 

I walk to my house. The sun is bright. I am very happy. I want to go home and eat my red apple. It is a very good day.
