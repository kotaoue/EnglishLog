## テーマ: Shopping at the Supermarket (スーパーマーケットでお買い物)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Saturday. It is a sunny day. I go to the supermarket with my mother. The supermarket is very big and clean. We need to buy food for one week.

First, we look at the fruits. I like apples, oranges, and bananas. My mother buys five big red apples. She also buys a bag of yellow bananas. They look very sweet and delicious.

Next, we go to the vegetable area. We see many colors. There are orange carrots, brown potatoes, and white onions. My mother picks up four big potatoes. She wants to make hot curry for dinner. I find some green broccoli. Broccoli is very healthy. We put it in our basket.

Then, we go to the dairy section. We need milk and eggs. We put a large carton of milk in our basket. We also get twelve eggs. The basket is heavy now.

After that, we look for bread and snacks. I like white bread for toast. My mother likes brown bread. We buy both. I also find some chocolate cookies. My mother says, "Okay, just one box." I am very happy.

Finally, we go to the cash register. There are many people. A kind clerk scans our food. My mother pays with her credit card. I help her carry the heavy bags to the car. Now we go home. It is time to cook dinner.
