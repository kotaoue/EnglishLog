## テーマ: The Importance of Good Sleep (良質な睡眠の重要性)

### 英文パッセージ

Sleep is one of the most important things for a healthy life. Many people today are very busy with work, school, and hobbies. Because of this, they often stay up late and do not get enough sleep. However, sleeping well is necessary for both our bodies and our minds.

First, sleep helps our bodies recover. When we are asleep, our bodies fix tired muscles and grow stronger. If you sleep for seven or eight hours, you will wake up feeling full of energy. This energy helps you stay active all day. Without enough sleep, you might feel tired and have a hard time finishing your tasks.

Second, sleep is important for our brains. While we sleep, our brains process the information we learned during the day. This makes it easier to remember new things. Students who sleep well often perform better on tests because their brains are well-rested.

Third, sleep affects our emotions. When we do not get enough rest, we can become easily stressed or annoyed. On the other hand, a good night’s sleep helps us stay calm and happy. It is much easier to be kind to others when we are not tired.

To improve your sleep, you can try a few simple things. For example, do not use your phone or computer for 30 minutes before bed. The light from these devices can keep your brain awake. Also, try to go to bed and wake up at the same time every day. These small habits can lead to a much healthier and happier life.
