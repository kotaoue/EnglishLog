## テーマ: The Joy of Reading Books (本を読む楽しさ)

### 英文パッセージ

Many people like reading books. Reading is a very good hobby. You can learn many new things from books. For example, you can learn about history, science, or other countries. Books can take you to a different world.

There are many kinds of books. Some people like storybooks. These are called fiction. Other people like books about real things. These are called non-fiction. Children like picture books because they have many colors and beautiful pictures.

Where do you read books? Some people read at home. They sit on a soft sofa and drink tea. Other people read on the train. It is a good way to use time. You can also go to the library. Libraries have many books, and you can borrow them for free.

Today, many people read e-books. E-books are books on a computer, a tablet, or a phone. They are very light and easy to carry. You can have hundreds of books in one small machine.

Reading is also good for your brain. It helps you relax. When you read a book before bed, you can sleep better. If you want to start reading, choose a book you like. You do not need to read fast. Just enjoy the story. Books are great friends for everyone.
