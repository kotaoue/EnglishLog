## テーマ: Cooking at Home (家で料理をすること)

### 英文パッセージ

Hello. My name is Ken. I like cooking at home. Many people eat at restaurants, but I like my kitchen. Cooking is a very fun hobby for me.

Today is Saturday. I have time, so I make a special dinner. I am making pasta today. I like pasta very much. First, I boil water in a big pot. Then, I put the pasta in the water. Next, I make the sauce. I use many tomatoes, onions, and some salt. The smell is very good. 

I also make a fresh salad. I use lettuce and carrots. Vegetables are very healthy for our bodies. Eating healthy food is important. 

At six o'clock, my family comes to the table. We eat dinner together. My mother says, "Ken, this pasta is great!" My father says, "The salad is very fresh." I am very happy to hear that. 

Cooking at home is good for three reasons. First, it is fun to make things. Second, it is healthy because we know what is inside the food. Third, it is not expensive. It is cheaper than a restaurant. 

Do you like cooking? It is not difficult. You can start with simple food. Cooking makes people smile. Let's try cooking today!
