## テーマ: The Benefits of Cooking at Home (自炊のメリット)

### 英文パッセージ

Many people today are very busy, so they often eat at restaurants or buy fast food. However, cooking at home has many great benefits.

First, cooking at home is good for your health. When you cook for yourself, you can choose fresh vegetables and good meat. You can also control the amount of salt and sugar in your meals. Restaurant food often has a lot of salt and oil, which can be bad for your body if you eat it every day.

Second, you can save money. Buying ingredients at a supermarket is usually cheaper than eating out. If you make a large meal, you can keep the leftovers for lunch the next day. This helps you save even more money.

Finally, cooking is a fun hobby. You can try new recipes from different countries. You might fail sometimes, but you will improve your skills over time. Making a delicious meal for your family or friends can make you feel very happy.

In conclusion, cooking at home is healthy, cheap, and fun. Why don't you start cooking tonight?
