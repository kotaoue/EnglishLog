## テーマ: The Joy of Having a Pet (ペットを飼う喜び)

### 英文パッセージ

Many people around the world have pets. Dogs and cats are very popular pets. Having a pet is a very good thing for many reasons. A pet is not just an animal; a pet is a friend.

I have a dog. His name is Max. Max is a small dog. He is brown and white. He is very friendly and kind. Every morning, Max wakes me up at seven o'clock. We go for a walk in the park near my house. Max likes to run on the green grass. He runs very fast. I feel very happy when I see him running.

When I come home from school or work, Max is always there at the door. He wags his tail quickly. He is very happy to see me. I say, "Hello, Max! How are you?" We play with a yellow ball in the garden. Playing with a pet is very fun. It helps me relax after a long day.

Pets need food and water to stay healthy. I give Max food every morning and evening. I also give him fresh water. Taking care of a pet is a big responsibility, but it is important. 

Pets love their owners very much. Owners love their pets, too. A pet is a member of the family. Pets make our lives much better. They give us love and joy every day. Having a pet is a truly wonderful thing.
