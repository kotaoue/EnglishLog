## テーマ: A Day at the Beach (海での一日)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Sunday. It is a very beautiful day. The sky is clear and the sun is bright. It is very hot outside. I go to the beach with my family. I go with my father, my mother, and my little sister. We go there by car. The beach is near my house.

The sea is big and blue. The sand is white and warm. I like the sea very much. I have a big blue bag. In my bag, I have a towel, a sun hat, and a bottle of water. My hat is yellow and my towel is red. My sister has a small green bag.

We arrive at the beach at ten o'clock in the morning. There are many people today. Some people are swimming in the sea. Some people are sitting on the sand and reading books. Children are playing with balls.

I walk to the water. The water is cool and feels very good. I see many small fish in the water. They are orange and silver. They swim very fast. I swim with my father for thirty minutes. It is very fun.

At twelve o'clock, we eat lunch under a big umbrella. My mother makes ham and egg sandwiches. They are very delicious. We also eat apples and drink cold orange juice.

In the afternoon, I play with the sand with my sister. We make a big sandcastle. My father helps us. We use a small bucket and a shovel. The sandcastle looks like a real castle.

At four o'clock, we go home by car. I am a little tired, but I am very happy. I love the beach. I want to go to the beach again next week.
