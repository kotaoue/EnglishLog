## テーマ: The Power of Reading Books (読書の力)

### 英文パッセージ

Reading books is a wonderful hobby for everyone. Many people like to read books in their free time. Why is reading so popular? It is because reading is very good for our brains and hearts.

First, we can learn many new things from books. For example, we can learn about history, science, or how to cook delicious food. Books give us knowledge from experts all over the world. Second, reading helps us relax. When we read an interesting story, we forget about our daily stress. It is like traveling to a new world without moving. Third, reading improves our language skills. We can learn many new words and see how to use them in sentences. This helps us speak and write better.

You do not need a lot of money to enjoy reading. You can go to a local library and borrow many books for free. Also, you can read anytime and anywhere. You can read on a train, in a park, or before you go to bed. Today, some people like paper books because they like the smell of paper. Other people like e-books on their tablets because they are light and easy to carry. Both styles are great ways to enjoy stories.

If you are busy, you do not have to read for a long time. You can start with just ten minutes a day. Reading a little every day makes a big difference in the future. Let's find a book you like and start your reading journey today!
