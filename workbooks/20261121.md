## テーマ: Cooking Dinner at Home（家で夕食を作る）

### 英文パッセージ

Hello. My name is Ken. Today is Sunday. I am at home with my family now. We are in the big kitchen. My father, my mother, and my sister are all here. We are cooking dinner together.

Dinner tonight is curry and rice. It is my favorite food. I like curry very much. My mother is busy. She cuts the vegetables with a knife. She cuts orange carrots and brown potatoes. She also cuts onions. My father is at the stove. He cooks the meat in a big pot. The meat is beef.

My sister, Yumi, is helpful too. She washes the plates and dishes in the sink. I want to help my family. I take the spoons and forks from the drawer. I put them on the dining table. I also put four glasses on the table for water.

The kitchen smells very good now. I am very hungry. My stomach is growling. Finally, my mother says, "Dinner is ready! Everyone, please come to the table."

We all sit down. The curry looks very delicious. We say, "It looks great!" and start eating. The curry is a little spicy, but the taste is wonderful. We talk about our school and work. My father tells a funny story, and we all laugh.

Eating with my family makes me very happy. After dinner, we drink hot tea together. We are very relaxed. It is a very nice and peaceful Sunday evening at my house.
