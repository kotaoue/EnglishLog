## テーマ: A Visit to the Park (公園への外出)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Sunday. The weather is very nice. The sun is shining, and the sky is blue. I go to the park with my family. My family has four people: my father, my mother, my sister, and me. We also have a dog. His name is Max. Max is a small brown dog. He is very happy today. He runs on the green grass and wags his tail.

Many people are in the park. Some people are sitting on benches. Some people are sitting under big trees. They are reading books or talking to their friends. Other people are eating lunch. There are many flowers in the park. I see red, yellow, and white flowers. They smell very good.

We find a good place under a big oak tree. My mother brings a large basket. Inside the basket, there are sandwiches, apples, and orange juice. We eat lunch together. The sandwiches have ham and cheese. They are very delicious. My father drinks coffee from a bottle.

After lunch, I play with my sister. Her name is Hana. We play with a yellow ball. Max likes the ball, too. He tries to catch the ball with his mouth. It is very funny to watch. My father takes many pictures with his camera. He wants to show the pictures to my grandmother.

In the afternoon, the sun starts to go down. We clean up our place and go home. I am a little tired because I walked a lot. However, I am very happy. The park is a great place to spend time with family. I want to go there again next Sunday. It was a wonderful day for everyone.
