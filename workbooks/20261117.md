## テーマ: A Sunny Day in the Park (公園での晴れた日)

### 英文パッセージ

It is Sunday today. The weather is very nice. The sun is big and yellow. The sky is blue. There are no clouds. I am in the park now. I see many things in the park.

There are big trees. The trees are green. There are many beautiful flowers near the trees. The flowers are red, pink, and yellow. I like these flowers very much.

I see a small dog. The dog is white and cute. It is running on the grass. A boy is with the dog. The boy is ten years old. He is very happy. He has a big ball. The ball is blue and white. He throws the ball, and the dog runs.

I see a cat too. The cat is black. It is under a big tree. The cat is sleeping now. It is very quiet. I do not want to wake the cat.

Many children are in the park. They are playing together. Some children are running fast. Some children are sitting on the green grass. The grass is soft.

I have a sandwich and an apple in my bag. It is my lunch. I sit on a long bench. The bench is brown. I eat my sandwich slowly. It is very good. I eat the red apple too.

I drink some water. The water is cold and fresh. I am very happy in the park. The park is a very good place. I like sunny days. I go home at five o'clock.
