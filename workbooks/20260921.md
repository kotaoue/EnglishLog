## テーマ: The Benefits of Learning a New Language (新しい言語を学ぶメリット)

### 英文パッセージ

Learning a new language is a great way to improve your life. Many people think it is only for students or travelers, but anyone can enjoy it. It is not just about memorizing words; it is a way to change how you see the world.

First, learning a language is good for your brain. When you learn new words and grammar, your brain becomes stronger. It is like exercise for your mind. Some studies show that speaking more than one language can keep your brain healthy as you get older. It helps you focus better on tasks as well.

Second, it helps you understand different cultures. When you study a language, you also learn about how people in other countries live and think. This makes you more open-minded. You can make new friends from all over the world and talk to them in their own language, which is a wonderful experience.

Third, it gives you more opportunities in your career. Today, many companies look for workers who can speak more than one language. If you can speak English, for example, you can find much more information on the internet and read books from different countries.

Today, there are many ways to learn. You can use apps on your smartphone, watch movies with subtitles, or listen to foreign music. You don't need to go to a classroom every day. Even ten minutes of practice a day can make a big difference over time. It might be difficult at first, but the more you practice, the more fun it becomes.
