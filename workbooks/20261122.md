## テーマ: Cooking Dinner at Home（お家で夕食作り）

### 英文パッセージ

Hello. My name is Ken. Today is Sunday. My family is at home. We are in the kitchen. We are making dinner. It is five o'clock. 

My mother is making soup. The soup has many vegetables. The soup is hot. My father is washing vegetables in the sink. He is washing orange carrots and yellow onions. 

I am helping my father. I am cutting the carrots on a board. I use a small knife. I am very careful. My sister, Hana, is setting the table. She puts four white plates and four spoons on the table. She also puts glasses for water. The table is big and brown.

The kitchen smells very good. We are very hungry. My stomach is growling. Dinner is ready at six o'clock. We sit down in our chairs. We say, "Thank you for the food." 

The soup is delicious. The vegetables are sweet and soft. We talk about our day. My father talks about his work. My mother talks about her book. I talk about my school. My father likes the soup very much. My mother is very happy. 

I like cooking with my family. It is very fun. After dinner, I wash the dishes. My sister helps me. She wipes the plates. Then, we watch a movie on TV. It is a very good Sunday.
