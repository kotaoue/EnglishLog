## テーマ: Shopping at the Supermarket (スーパーでのお買い物)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Sunday. It is a sunny day. I go to the supermarket with my mother. The supermarket is near my house. It is a very big store. There are many things in the store.

First, we look at the fruits. There are many apples, oranges, and bananas. The apples are red. I like apples very much. My mother puts five apples in the basket. Then, we see the vegetables. We buy carrots and potatoes. We want to make curry for dinner.

Next, we go to the drinks area. We need milk and juice. My mother likes tea, but I like orange juice. We buy a big bottle of juice. We also buy some fish and some meat. The meat is for dinner. The fish is for my cat. My cat loves fish.

The supermarket is very busy today. I see many people. I see my friend, Tanaka-kun. He is with his sister. They are buying bread. I say, "Hi, Tanaka-kun!" He smiles at me.

Finally, we go to the register. A kind woman works there. My mother pays money. I help my mother with the bags. There are three bags. One bag is very heavy. 

We walk back to our house. I am a little tired, but I am happy. We will eat the apples for lunch. Shopping is fun!
