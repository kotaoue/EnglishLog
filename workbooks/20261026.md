## テーマ: My Daily Routine (私の毎日の生活)

### 英文パッセージ

Hello! My name is Ken. I live in Tokyo. I am a student. Every day, I wake up at 7:00. I am happy in the morning. 

First, I drink water. It is good for my body. Then, I wash my face with cold water. It feels cold but good. At 7:30, I eat breakfast with my family. We eat eggs and bread. I like eggs very much. My mother makes coffee. The coffee smells very nice.

After breakfast, I brush my teeth. I want to have clean teeth. Then, I put on my clothes. I wear a white shirt and blue pants today. At 8:30, I go to school. I walk to school with my friend, Tom. Tom is a very kind person. We talk about many things. We talk about soccer and games.

School starts at 9:00. I study English and math. English is fun for me. I want to speak English well. At noon, I eat lunch at school. I eat a sandwich. The sandwich has ham and cheese. It is very delicious.

In the afternoon, I play soccer with my friends. Soccer is my favorite sport. I run a lot. I feel tired, but I have a lot of fun. I go home at 5:00. I take a bath and relax. Then, I have dinner. We eat rice and fish. After dinner, I do my homework. I read a book before I go to sleep. I go to bed at 10:00. I like my daily routine.
