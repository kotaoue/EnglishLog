## テーマ: The Four Seasons (四季)

### 英文パッセージ

Japan has four seasons. They are spring, summer, fall, and winter. Each season is special and different. We can see many beautiful things in each season.

Spring comes in March and April. The weather becomes warm. Flowers bloom in the parks and gardens. Sakura, or cherry blossoms, are very famous and beautiful. Many people go to the park with their friends and family. They sit under the trees and eat lunch together. This is a very happy time for many people.

Summer starts in June and ends in August. It is very hot and sunny. Children like summer because they have a long vacation. They go to the sea or the mountains. They swim in the blue water. They also eat cold ice cream and watermelon. It is a very fun season for kids.

Fall is between September and November. The weather is cool and nice. The leaves on the trees change their colors. They become bright red, orange, and yellow. Fall is a good time for walking and sports. Many people also like to eat delicious food like sweet potatoes and apples.

Winter comes in December and stays until February. It is cold. In some places, it snows a lot. The ground becomes white. Children play outside and make snowmen. People wear warm coats, hats, and gloves. Hot soup and hot tea are very good in winter.

Which season do you like best? Every season has a different beauty. Let's enjoy each season!
