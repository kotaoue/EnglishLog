## テーマ: A Day at the Zoo (動物園での一日)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is a holiday. My name is Ken, and I am a student. Today, I go to the zoo with my family. I go with my father, my mother, and my younger sister. The weather is very nice. The sun is shining, and it is warm.

We arrive at the zoo at ten o'clock. There are many people. First, we see the lions. Lions are very strong. They have big teeth. One lion is walking, and two lions are sleeping. My sister says, "Look! The lions are cool."

Next, we go to see the elephants. Elephants are very big and gray. They have very long noses and big ears. They are drinking water. My father takes many photos with his camera.

After that, we see the monkeys. Monkeys are very funny. They like bananas. They are climbing the trees. I like monkeys because they are very active.

At twelve o'clock, we have lunch. We sit on a bench. My mother makes delicious sandwiches for us. I also eat an orange. It is very sweet.

In the afternoon, we see the penguins. They are black and white. They are swimming in the water. They are very cute.

We go home at four o'clock. I am a little tired, but I am very happy. It is a great day. I love animals.
