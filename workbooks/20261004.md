## テーマ: The Fun of Listening to Music (音楽を聴く楽しさ)

### 英文パッセージ

Music is everywhere in our lives. Many people like to listen to music every day. You can listen to music at home, on the bus, or while you walk in a park. Today, it is very easy to enjoy music because we have smartphones and earphones. You can carry thousands of songs in your pocket.

There are many different kinds of music in the world. For example, some people like pop music. They like it because it is catchy and the rhythm is fun to dance to. Other people prefer classical music. They like the sound of the piano or the violin. It helps them study or relax because it is calm and beautiful. Some people love rock music. They like the loud sound of the drums and electric guitars. It makes them feel strong and powerful.

Music has the power to change our feelings. When you are sad, listening to a slow and gentle song can make you feel better. When you are tired in the morning, a fast and upbeat song can give you energy to start the day.

Some people do not just listen to music; they also play instruments. They might play the piano, the guitar, or the drums. Playing an instrument is a great hobby. It takes a lot of practice, but it is very exciting when you can play your favorite song perfectly.

Finally, music brings people together. We go to concerts with our friends and family. We sing songs together at parties. Even if people speak different languages, they can enjoy the same music. Music is a universal language that makes our lives much happier.
