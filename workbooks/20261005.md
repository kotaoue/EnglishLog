## テーマ: The Fun of Cooking at Home (家で料理をする楽しさ)

### 英文パッセージ

Cooking is a great hobby. Many people like cooking at home. I like it too. I cook in my kitchen every day.

In the morning, I wake up and go to the kitchen. I make a simple breakfast. I fry eggs and toast some bread. I also drink a cup of hot coffee. It is a good start to the day.

In the evening, I cook dinner for my family. My family is very happy when I cook. My mother loves my tomato pasta. She says it is delicious. My father likes my green salad with many vegetables. I use carrots, tomatoes, and cucumbers. Fresh vegetables are very healthy. 

Cooking at home is good for two reasons. First, it is healthy. You can choose good food. Second, it is cheap. Eating at a restaurant costs a lot of money. Cooking at home saves money.

Sometimes, I invite my friends to my house. We cook lunch together. We make pizza or sandwiches. We talk about many things and laugh a lot. It is a very fun time. 

I have a dream. I want to learn many new recipes. Next month is my sister’s birthday. I want to make a big chocolate cake for her. I want to see her smile. Cooking is not just about food. It is about making people happy.
