## テーマ: My Morning Routine (私の朝の習慣)

### 英文パッセージ

Good morning! My name is Ken. I am a junior high school student. I live in a small house with my father, my mother, and my cat.

Every morning, I wake up at six thirty. First, I open the window. The sun is bright. I wash my face with cold water. It feels good. Then, I go to the kitchen. My mother is there. She is cooking breakfast. We eat breakfast at seven o'clock. I eat rice, miso soup, and fish today. I like Japanese food very much. My father drinks hot coffee. He looks at his phone.

After breakfast, I brush my teeth in the bathroom. Then I put on my school clothes. I look at the big clock in the living room. It is seven forty-five. It is time to go. 

I find my school bag near the door. My cat, Luna, is sleeping on the chair. Luna is a very cute cat. She has white hair and blue eyes. I touch her head and say, "See you, Luna." 

I walk to the bus stop. The bus stop is near my house. I wait for the bus for five minutes. The bus comes at eight o'clock. It is a big blue bus. I get on the bus and sit down. I see my friend, Tom, on the bus. We talk about a new video game. 

I arrive at school at eight thirty. I am ready for my classes. I want to study English today. It is a good morning.
