## テーマ: The Benefits of Reading Books (読書のメリット)

### 英文パッセージ

Reading books is a very good habit. Many people think reading is difficult, but it is actually fun. There are three main reasons why reading is good for you.

First, reading helps you learn new words. When you read a book, you see many interesting words. You can see how to use them in sentences. This is very good for students and workers. If you read every day, your vocabulary will grow.

Second, reading is like exercise for your brain. When you read a story, you imagine the characters and the places. You think about what happens next. This makes your brain strong and healthy. It is better than just watching television because you have to use your mind.

Third, reading helps you relax. Today, many people are very busy. They use their smartphones and computers all the time. But a book is quiet. You can sit on a sofa and enjoy a story. This makes you feel calm and happy. 

You do not need to read a big, difficult book. You can start with a short story or a picture book. Reading for just fifteen minutes every day is great. You can read in the morning or before you go to sleep. Books can take you to different worlds. So, let's go to a library or a bookstore today. Find a book you like and start reading!
