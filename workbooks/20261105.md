## テーマ: A Day at the Beach (海での一日)

### 英文パッセージ

I go to the beach. The sun is hot today. The sky is very blue and clear. I am with my brother. My brother’s name is Ken. We see the ocean. The ocean is very big. I have a big red ball. We play with the ball on the sand. The sand is yellow. Ken runs to the water. I swim in the water. The water is cold but it is good. We are very happy.

It is lunch time now. We sit on the sand and eat. We eat sandwiches. We drink orange juice. The food is very good. We see a small bird. The bird is white. It flies in the sky. There are many people at the beach. Some people sleep under the sun. Some people walk with their dogs. 

In the afternoon, we make a sandcastle. It is a big castle. We use a blue bucket and a small shovel. We see many shells on the sand, too. The shells are beautiful. Ken picks up a pink shell. I pick up a brown shell. We put the shells in our bag. We will show the shells to our mother at home.

Now it is five o’clock. The sun is going down. We go to our car. We are tired, but we are very happy. The beach is a great place. I like the beach very much.
