## テーマ: The Importance of Sleep (睡眠の大切さ)

### 英文パッセージ

Sleep is a vital part of our daily lives, yet many people do not get enough of it. In our modern world, we are often busy with work, school, and social media. We sometimes think that staying up late to finish a project or watch a movie is okay. However, science shows that sleep is just as important as eating healthy food and exercising regularly.

Our bodies and brains do a lot of work while we are asleep. First, sleep helps our brain function properly. During the night, the brain processes the information we learned during the day. This helps improve our memory and problem-solving skills. Without enough sleep, it becomes difficult to make decisions or stay creative.

Second, sleep is essential for physical health. It is the time when our body repairs its tissues, muscles, and heart. For example, if you exercise hard, your muscles grow and recover while you sleep. People who sleep well also have a stronger immune system, which means they do not get sick as often.

Health experts suggest that most adults need between seven and nine hours of sleep each night. If you constantly sleep less than this, you may experience "sleep deprivation." This can make you feel tired, grumpy, and unable to concentrate. Over time, lack of sleep can lead to serious health problems.

To improve your sleep quality, you can follow a few simple tips. It is helpful to have a regular sleep schedule by going to bed and waking up at the same time every day. Also, avoid using electronic devices like smartphones at least one hour before bed. By prioritizing sleep, you can enjoy a healthier and happier life.
