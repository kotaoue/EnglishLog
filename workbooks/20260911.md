## テーマ: The Future of Remote Work (リモートワークの未来)

### 英文パッセージ

Remote work has become a normal part of life for many people around the world. Before the pandemic, most employees went to an office every day. However, technology like high-speed internet and video calls changed everything. Now, many companies are deciding how to work in the future.

Many workers enjoy working from home. They say it saves time because they do not have to commute. Instead of spending an hour on a crowded train, they can start working immediately or spend more time with their families. This flexibility often leads to higher productivity. Also, people can work from different cities or even different countries.

However, remote work also has challenges. Some people feel lonely without seeing their colleagues in person. It can also be difficult to separate work life from home life. When your office is in your living room, you might work too many hours without a break. Furthermore, communication can sometimes be misunderstood through emails or chat messages.

To solve these problems, many companies are choosing a "hybrid" model. This means employees work in the office for two or three days and work from home for the rest of the week. This way, people can enjoy both the flexibility of home and the social connection of the office. The future of work is not just about where we sit, but how we use technology to balance our lives.
