## テーマ: The Joy of Cooking at Home（家で料理をする楽しさ）

### 英文パッセージ

Do you like to cook? Many people like to cook at home. Cooking at home has many good points. First, it is healthy. You can choose fresh vegetables and good meat. You can use less salt and sugar. This is good for your body. Second, it is cheap. Restaurants are often expensive. If you buy food at a supermarket, you can save money.

I cook every day. In the morning, I make breakfast. I usually eat eggs and toast. I also drink orange juice. In the evening, I make dinner for my family. My favorite dish is curry and rice. My children love it very much. They always say, "This is delicious!" That makes me very happy.

Sometimes cooking is difficult. It takes time to wash and cut vegetables. You also have to wash the dishes after eating. But I think it is worth it. I feel relaxed when I cook. I like the sound of the pan and the smell of the food. 

Next weekend, I want to try a new recipe. I want to make Italian pasta. I will buy tomatoes, garlic, and cheese. I will invite my friends to my house. We will eat together and talk a lot. Cooking is a great way to show love to your family and friends.
