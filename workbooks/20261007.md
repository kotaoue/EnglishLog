## テーマ: The Benefits of Walking (歩くことの良さ)

### 英文パッセージ

Walking is a simple way to stay healthy. Many people like walking because it is easy. You do not need a lot of money to start. You only need a pair of shoes and a place to walk. You can walk in a park, on a street, or near your house.

Walking helps your body. It is good for your heart. If you walk every day, you can become strong. Some people walk to lose weight. It is a very safe exercise for people of all ages. Both young people and old people can enjoy it.

Walking also helps your mind. When you are stressed, you can go outside. You can see the green trees and the blue sky. The fresh air makes you feel better. Many people think of good ideas while they are walking. It is a quiet time to relax.

You can walk alone, or you can walk with someone. Walking with a friend is fun. You can talk about your day. Some people like to walk with their dogs. The dog will be happy, and you will be happy, too.

You do not need to walk for many hours. Even a short walk is good. You can walk for fifteen or twenty minutes. For example, you can walk to a store instead of using a car. 

Walking is a great hobby. It is good for your body and your mind. Why don't you go for a walk today? It is a small step, but it is very important for your life.
