## テーマ: Digital Detox (デジタル・デトックス)

### 英文パッセージ

In today’s world, we use smartphones and computers for almost everything. They are very useful for work, study, and staying in touch with friends. However, many experts say that using these devices too much can be bad for our health. This is why "digital detox" is becoming popular.

A digital detox means taking a break from electronic devices for a certain period of time. When we spend too much time on social media or checking emails, our brains become very tired. This constant connection can cause stress and make it difficult to focus on important tasks. Also, looking at screens late at night often makes it hard to sleep well.

There are simple ways to start a digital detox. For example, you can decide not to use your phone during meals. Another way is to turn off all devices one hour before you go to bed. Instead of looking at a screen, you can read a physical book, listen to music, or talk with your family.

The goal of a digital detox is not to stop using technology forever. Instead, it is about finding a healthy balance. By spending less time on screens, many people find that they feel more relaxed and can enjoy the real world around them more clearly.
