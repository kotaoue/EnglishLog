## テーマ: My Morning Routine (私の朝のルーティン)

### 英文パッセージ

Hello! My name is Ken. I want to tell you about my morning routine. Every day, I wake up at 7:00 AM. First, I drink a large glass of water. It is very good for my health. Then, I go to the bathroom to wash my face and brush my teeth.

At 7:30 AM, it is time for breakfast. I usually eat toast, an egg, and some fruit. I also drink a cup of hot coffee. I love the smell of coffee; it helps me wake up. While I eat, I look at the news on my smartphone.

After breakfast, I get ready for work. I put on my suit and take my black bag. I leave my house at 8:15 AM. I walk to the train station near my house. The walk takes about ten minutes. If the weather is sunny, I feel very happy during my walk.

I take the train to my office. On the train, it is often crowded, but I like to listen to music or read a short book. It is a quiet time for me before work starts. I arrive at my office at 9:00 AM. I say "Good morning" to my coworkers. A good morning routine helps me have a great day.
