## テーマ: The Magic of Cooking at Home (家で料理をすることの魅力)

### 英文パッセージ

Cooking at home is becoming very popular these days. Many people prefer to make their own meals instead of eating at restaurants. There are several important reasons for this change.

First, cooking at home is much cheaper. Eating out can be expensive, especially if you go to a restaurant often. When you buy ingredients at a supermarket, you can save a lot of money. You can also use the same ingredients for different meals, which helps you reduce waste.

Second, home-cooked meals are usually healthier. When you cook for yourself, you know exactly what is in your food. You can choose fresh vegetables and control the amount of salt and sugar. Restaurants often use a lot of oil and salt to make the food taste better, but this is not always good for your body.

Third, cooking can be a fun and creative hobby. Trying new recipes is like a small adventure in your kitchen. You can change the taste to fit your own preference. Many people also find that cooking helps them relax after a busy day at work. Sharing a meal you made with your family or friends is a great way to show your love and spend time together.

In conclusion, cooking at home is good for your wallet, your health, and your mind. It is a simple way to improve your daily life. Even if you are busy, starting with easy recipes can be a good first step.
