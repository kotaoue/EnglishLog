## テーマ: The Rise of Urban Gardening (都市菜園の普及)

### 英文パッセージ

Many people live in big cities today. In these cities, there are many tall buildings and busy streets. However, more people are starting "urban gardens." An urban garden is a small space where people grow vegetables and flowers in the city. You can find them on rooftops, balconies, or in small parks. These green areas are changing how people live in crowded places.

There are many reasons why urban gardening is becoming more popular. First, it helps people eat healthy food. When you grow your own vegetables, you know they are fresh and natural. Many city shops sell expensive vegetables, so growing your own can also save money. Second, it is very good for the environment. Plants produce oxygen and make the city air cleaner. They also help keep buildings cool during hot summers. This is important because cities often become very hot due to all the concrete and cars.

Finally, urban gardening is good for the community. In a busy city, people often do not know their neighbors. However, in a community garden, people work together and talk to each other. It is a great way to make new friends and share tips about gardening. Even though city life is often stressful, these small green spaces give people a place to relax and enjoy nature. Experts believe that urban gardening will continue to grow as more people look for a balance between city life and the natural world.
