## テーマ: Going to the Beach (海に行くこと)

### 英文パッセージ

It is Sunday. The weather is very good today. The sun is bright and the sky is blue. I go to the beach with my family. We go there by car. My father drives the car. My mother sits in the front seat. My brother and I sit in the back seat. We are very happy.

The beach is very big. The water is blue and beautiful. I see many people there. Some people swim in the sea. Some people play with a ball. Some people sit under big umbrellas. 

I like the sand. The sand is hot. I make a sandcastle with my brother. My brother is small, but he is a good helper. We make a big castle. It is very fun. 

My mother has some food in a bag. She has sandwiches, apples, and water. We sit on a big blue towel. We eat our lunch together. The sandwiches are very good. I like apples, too. 

In the afternoon, we walk on the beach. I find some sea shells. They are small and white. I put them in my bag. I want to show them to my friends. 

The sun goes down. The sky is now orange. It is very beautiful. We go to the car at five o'clock. We go home. I am a little tired, but I am very happy. It is a great day. I want to go to the beach again.
