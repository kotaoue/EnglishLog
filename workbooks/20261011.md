## テーマ: The Joy of Having a Pet (ペットを飼う楽しさ)

### 英文パッセージ

I have a dog. His name is Lucky. Lucky is a big brown dog. I like dogs very much. Lucky is my best friend.

Every morning, I wake up at seven o'clock. Lucky waits for me. We go for a walk in the park. The park is near my house. In the park, Lucky runs and jumps. He is very happy. Then, we come home. I give him food and water. Lucky eats his breakfast fast. He likes chicken and rice. I have breakfast, too. I drink coffee and eat some bread.

In the evening, I play with Lucky. We play with a ball. I throw the ball, and Lucky catches it. He is a very smart dog. Sometimes, we sit on the sofa and watch TV together.

Keeping a pet is not always easy. I must clean the house every day. I must wash Lucky once a month. But I am never lonely. Lucky is always with me. When I am sad, Lucky sits next to me. He makes me smile.

On weekends, my family goes to the mountains with Lucky. There are many trees and flowers. Lucky loves the forest. It is good exercise for me and my dog. Many people have pets in their houses. Some people have cats, and some people have birds. Having an animal is very good for us. Lucky is a part of my family. I love him very much.
