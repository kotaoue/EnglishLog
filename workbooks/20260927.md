## テーマ: The Fun of Traveling (旅行の楽しさ)

### 英文パッセージ

Many people like to travel. Traveling is fun and exciting. When we go to a new place, we can see many things. For example, we can see beautiful mountains, blue oceans, or big cities. We can also meet new people. Talking to local people is very interesting.

Eating new food is a big part of traveling. Every place has its own special food. Sometimes the food is spicy, and sometimes it is sweet. Trying new tastes makes us happy. We can also buy souvenirs for our friends and family. Souvenirs help us remember the trip.

Traveling is good for our health, too. It helps us relax. When we are away from work or school, we do not feel stressed. We can sleep a lot and walk in nature. This makes our mind and body strong. 

Some people like to travel alone. Other people like to travel with friends or family. Traveling alone is quiet and peaceful. Traveling with friends is noisy but very fun. Both are great ways to spend time.

Taking photos is important. We can look at the photos later. They remind us of the fun times. Even if a trip is short, it can be a great memory. Where do you want to go next? Planning a trip is also very exciting.
