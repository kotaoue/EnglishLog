## テーマ: A Birthday Party（お誕生会）

### 英文パッセージ

Today is a special day. It is Saturday. Today is my birthday. I am very happy. I wake up at seven o'clock. I see my mother and father. They say, "Happy Birthday!" I say, "Thank you."

In the afternoon, my friends come to my house. I have four friends. Their names are Ken, Yumi, Hana, and Tom. They bring colorful boxes. The boxes are presents. We go to the living room. 

We see a big table. There is a lot of food on the table. We have pizza, sandwiches, and fruit. We have orange juice and apple juice. I like orange juice. My friend Ken likes apple juice. The food is very good.

Next, my mother brings the cake. It is a big chocolate cake. There are ten red candles on the cake. I am ten years old today. My friends sing a song. They sing, "Happy Birthday to You." I am a little shy, but I am happy. I blow out the candles. Everyone claps their hands. 

We eat the cake. It is sweet and delicious. Then we go outside. We play with a soccer ball in the garden. The sun is shining. It is a warm day. We run and jump.

At five o'clock, my friends go home. I say, "Goodbye!" I look at my presents. I have a new book, a blue pen, and a cool toy car. Today is a great day. I love my birthday.
