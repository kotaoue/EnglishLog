## テーマ: The Joy of Music (音楽の楽しさ)

### 英文パッセージ

Music is a big part of our daily life. Almost everyone in the world listens to some kind of music. I love music very much. I listen to my favorite songs every morning. It helps me wake up. When I am tired, I listen to quiet music. It helps me relax.

In the past, people listened to music on the radio or CDs. Now, many people use their smartphones. We can listen to millions of songs anytime and anywhere. This is very convenient. You can listen to music while you walk or while you work.

Playing music is also a wonderful hobby. My brother plays the guitar. He practices every evening in his room. He wants to be in a band. I want to play the piano someday. Learning a new instrument is difficult, but it is very exciting.

Music has a special power. It can change our mood. When we are sad, a beautiful song can make us feel better. When we are happy, we want to dance. Music is for everyone. It does not matter how old you are. Music connects people all over the world. Let’s listen to music and have a good day.
