## テーマ: A Visit to the City Library（市立図書館への訪問）

### 英文パッセージ

Last Saturday, I went to the city library with my younger brother, Ken. The library is a large, white building near the central park. It is a very quiet and peaceful place. We arrived there at ten o'clock in the morning.

When we entered the building, we saw many bookshelves. There are thousands of books about history, science, and cooking. First, we went to the children's section. Ken loves animals, so he looked for a book about elephants. He found a big book with many beautiful pictures. He sat on a small chair and started reading it happily.

Next, I went to the computer section. I wanted to learn about famous cities in Europe because I want to travel there someday. I used a computer to look for some information about Paris and London. The library also has many magazines and newspapers. I saw some people drinking coffee and reading newspapers in the cafe area.

At twelve o'clock, we felt a little hungry. There is a small garden behind the library. We went outside and ate our sandwiches under a big tree. The weather was sunny and warm. After lunch, we returned the books we borrowed last week and checked out three new books. 

We stayed at the library for three hours. I think the library is a great place to learn new things and relax. We want to go there again next weekend.
