## テーマ: A Visit to the Zoo (動物園への訪問)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Sunday. It is a sunny day. I go to the zoo with my family. My father, my mother, and my little sister are with me. We are very happy. 

First, we see the elephants. They are very big and grey. They have long noses. The elephants are eating grass. My sister says, "Look! They are so cool!" 

Next, we see the lions. There are three lions. They are sleeping under a big tree. Lions are the kings of the forest, but today they look like big cats. They are very cute. 

Then, we go to see the giraffes. Giraffes have very long necks. They eat leaves from tall trees. They are yellow and brown. I like giraffes very much. 

At noon, we eat lunch. My mother made sandwiches for us. We sit on a bench and eat. The sandwiches are very delicious. 

After lunch, we see the monkeys. The monkeys are very active. They jump and run. Some monkeys are eating bananas. We laugh because they are funny. 

It is now three o'clock. We are tired but very happy. We go home by car. The zoo is a very fun place. I want to go to the zoo again.
