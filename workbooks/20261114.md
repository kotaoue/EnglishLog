## テーマ: My Birthday Party (私の誕生日会)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Saturday. It is my birthday. I am happy today. My name is Ken. I am ten years old.

It is sunny outside. My friends come to my house at two o'clock. Their names are Tom and Hana. Tom has a blue hat. Hana has a yellow flower. We say, "Hello!" 

In the living room, there is a big table. On the table, there is a big cake. The cake is white and red. It has ten candles. The cake is very sweet and delicious. We eat the cake together. We drink orange juice.

I have many presents on the chair. My father gives me a new ball. It is a red soccer ball. I like soccer very much. My mother gives me a new book. The book has many pictures of animals. I like cats and dogs.

We sing the "Happy Birthday" song. We play games in the garden. The sun is bright. We laugh and run.

Now it is five o'clock. My friends go home. I say, "Goodbye. Thank you for coming." I am tired, but I am very happy. It is a great day.
