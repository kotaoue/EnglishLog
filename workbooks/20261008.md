## テーマ: The Benefits of Walking (歩くことのメリット)

### 英文パッセージ

Walking is a simple and great way to stay healthy. You do not need expensive tools to walk. You only need a pair of comfortable shoes. Many people like walking because it is easy and free. You can walk in a park, on the street, or even in a big store.

Walking has many benefits for your body. First, it makes your heart strong. When you walk every day, your heart works better. Second, walking helps you lose weight. It burns calories and makes your muscles strong. Third, it is good for your mind. If you feel stressed, a short walk outside can make you feel happy. You can see trees, flowers, and the blue sky.

It is a good idea to walk for 30 minutes every day. You do not have to walk fast. A slow walk is also good for you. You can walk with your friends or family. Talking while walking is a lot of fun. You can also listen to music.

If you are busy, you can start with 10 minutes. For example, you can walk to the station or a convenience store. Little steps are very important. Let’s start walking today for a healthy life!
