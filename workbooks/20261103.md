## テーマ: My Family and Breakfast (私の家族と朝食)

### 英文パッセージ

Hello. My name is Ken. I am a student. I live with my family. There are four people in my family. They are my father, my mother, my little sister, and me. We also have a small dog. His name is Pochi. Pochi is very cute.

Every morning, I get up at seven o'clock. My mother gets up at six o'clock. She goes to the kitchen. She makes breakfast for us. My father gets up at seven thirty. He is very busy. 

Today is Sunday. We are in the kitchen now. We are eating breakfast together. I am eating bread and an egg. My father is drinking coffee. My mother is drinking tea. My sister, Sakura, is eating an apple. She likes apples very much. Pochi is under the table. He is hungry, too.

After breakfast, we clean the room. My father washes the dishes. My mother cleans the floor. Sakura and I help them. We are a happy family. I love my family very much.
