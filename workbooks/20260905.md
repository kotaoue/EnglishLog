## テーマ: Recycling at Home (家庭でのリサイクル)

### 英文パッセージ

Taking care of our planet is very important. One of the easiest ways to help the environment is recycling. Many people think recycling is difficult, but it is actually very simple to do at home. You can start small and make it a habit.

First, you should look at the trash you make every day. Most of it is paper, plastic, glass, or cans. You can separate these items into different bins. For example, you can put empty water bottles in the plastic bin and old newspapers in the paper bin. This helps because these materials can be used again to make new things. If we recycle, we don't need to take more resources from nature.

Recycling also helps reduce the amount of waste in the world. When we throw everything away, it goes to big trash sites called landfills. These landfills get very full and are bad for the earth. By recycling, we save space and protect nature.

Another great way to help is to reuse things. Before you throw something away, ask yourself: "Can I use this again?" For example, a glass jar can become a pencil holder. A small action can make a big difference. Many cities have special days to collect recycling. You should check the schedule for your neighborhood. Let’s start recycling today to keep our world beautiful and clean for the future.
