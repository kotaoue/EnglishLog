## テーマ: A Visit to the Library (図書館への訪問)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Saturday. The weather is very nice and sunny. I go to the library in my town. The library is a large building near the park. I like the library because it is very quiet inside. I see many people there. Some people are reading newspapers. Some children are looking at colorful picture books.

I like books very much. I walk to a tall shelf. There are many books on the shelf. I look for a book about animals. I like cats and dogs. Finally, I find a beautiful book about cats. The book is small and thin. It has many pictures of cute cats. 

I take the book to a wooden table. I sit on a brown chair. The chair is soft and comfortable. I read the book for thirty minutes. The stories in the book are very interesting and easy. 

My friend, Tom, comes to the library too. He has a big bag. He wants to study English today. We see each other and say "Hello" very quietly. We do not speak loudly in the library because people are studying.

I want to read this book at my house. I take the book to the big desk near the door. A kind woman works there. I give her my library card. She smiles and says, "Thank you. Please enjoy the book." Now I can take the book home. I am very happy. I walk back home under the blue sky. I want to go to the library again next week.
