## テーマ: The Benefits of Walking (ウォーキングのメリット)

### 英文パッセージ

Walking is one of the easiest ways to stay healthy. Many people walk every day to go to work or school. However, taking a walk just for exercise is also very good for our body and mind.

First, walking helps our body. It makes our heart strong and helps us keep a healthy weight. Doctors say that walking for 30 minutes every day is good for us. You do not need expensive clothes or special tools to start. You only need a pair of good shoes.

Second, walking is good for our mental health. When we walk outside, we can see trees, flowers, and the sky. This helps us feel relaxed and reduces stress. If you feel tired or sad, a short walk in a park can make you feel better. 

Finally, walking is a great way to think. Many famous people in history liked to walk when they had difficult problems to solve. Walking helps the brain work better and gives us new ideas.

In conclusion, walking is a simple but powerful habit. It is good for your heart, your mind, and your brain. Why don't you start walking today?
