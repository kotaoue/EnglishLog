## テーマ: My Best Friend: My Dog Max (私の親友：犬のマックス)

### 英文パッセージ

Hello. My name is Ken. I have a dog. His name is Max. Max is my best friend.

Max is a big dog. He is brown and white. He has long ears and a short tail. He is very cute. Max lives with my family. We live in a small house. Max has a bed in the living room. It is a blue bed. He sleeps on his bed every night.

In the morning, Max wakes me up. He licks my face. It is funny. I say, "Good morning, Max!" I give him food and water. Max likes dog food. He eats a lot. He is a hungry dog.

After school, I play with Max. We go to the garden. I have a yellow ball. I throw the ball. Max runs fast. He catches the ball. He brings the ball to me. He is a smart dog. 

Sometimes, my father takes Max for a walk. Max likes to walk. He looks at the birds. He looks at the cats. He is happy. 

In the evening, Max is tired. He sits with me on the sofa. I pet his head. He closes his eyes. Max is a part of my family. I love Max very much. Having a dog is great. Max makes me happy every day.
