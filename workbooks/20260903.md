## テーマ: The Importance of Drinking Water (水を飲むことの大切さ)

### 英文パッセージ

Water is very important for our health. About 60% of the human body is made of water. Every part of our body needs water to work correctly. For example, water helps our brain stay focused and gives us energy. It also helps our skin look healthy. It is essential for our digestion and keeps our body temperature normal.

Many people do not drink enough water every day. When you do not drink enough, you feel tired. You may also get a headache. This is called dehydration. To stay healthy, doctors say we should drink about two liters of water every day. However, this amount depends on the person and the weather. If it is a hot day or if you exercise, you need to drink more water than usual.

It is sometimes difficult to remember to drink water during a busy day. Here are some simple tips to help you. First, carry a reusable water bottle with you. You can take a small sip whenever you are thirsty. Second, try to drink a glass of water right after you wake up in the morning. Third, eat fruits and vegetables like watermelon or cucumbers because they contain a lot of water.

Drinking water is a simple habit, but it has big benefits for your life. Instead of drinking sugary soda or energy drinks, choose water. Your body will feel much better!
