## テーマ: A Sunny Day at the Park (公園での晴れた一日)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is a sunny day. The sun is big and bright. I go to the park. The park is very big. I see many green trees. I see many flowers, too. Some flowers are red, and some flowers are yellow. They are very beautiful.

I see a dog in the park. The dog is small and white. Its name is Yuki. Yuki runs very fast on the grass. A boy plays with Yuki. The boy is happy. He has a blue ball. He throws the ball, and Yuki runs to it.

There is a big lake in the park. The water is blue and clean. I see three ducks in the lake. They are brown. They swim slowly.

I sit on a bench. The bench is under a big tree. It is cool there. I eat a sandwich and drink water. The sandwich is good. I look at the sky. The sky is clear.

Many children are in the park. They run and jump. They are very happy. I like this park. It is a quiet and nice place. I want to go to the park again. It is a very good day.
