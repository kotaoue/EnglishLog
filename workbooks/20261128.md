## テーマ: A Day at the Zoo（動物園での一日）

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Sunday. I go to the zoo with my father and mother. The zoo is a very big place. There are many trees and flowers. The weather is very nice today. It is sunny.

First, we look at the lions. Lions are big and strong. They are yellow and brown. One lion is eating meat. Two lions are sleeping under a tree. Next, we go to see the elephants. Elephants are very big and grey. They have very long noses. We call the long nose a "trunk." They use their trunks to drink water.

Then, we see the monkeys. The monkeys are small and brown. They are very fast. They climb the trees and eat bananas. They look very happy. I like monkeys because they are funny. After that, we see the giraffes. Giraffes have very long necks. They can eat leaves from tall trees. They are quiet and tall animals. My mother likes giraffes very much.

At twelve o’clock, we sit on a bench and eat lunch. I have a ham sandwich and orange juice. My father has a big piece of bread. We talk and smile. We are very happy. 

Finally, we go to the gift shop. I see many toys. I buy a small tiger toy. It is soft and orange. At four o'clock, we walk to our car. I am a little tired, but I am very happy. It is a great day. I want to go to the zoo again.
