## テーマ: Online Shopping (オンラインショッピング)

### 英文パッセージ

Hello, my name is Ken. I live in a small town. I love shopping online. Every month, I buy many things from websites on the internet. Last week, I bought a new blue shirt and a book about Italian cooking. I like online shopping because it is very easy and convenient. I do not need to go to a big store or wait in line. I can stay at home, drink tea, and use my computer or my smartphone.

First, I search for the items I want to buy. I look at the pictures and check the prices. Some items are cheap, and some are expensive. I read the reviews from other people to see if the product is good. Then, I put the items in my digital cart. I pay with my credit card. The process is very fast and simple.

Next, I wait for my package. Usually, the package arrives at my door in two or three days. A delivery person brings it to my house. Sometimes, I buy groceries online too. I buy things like milk, eggs, and fresh bread. This saves me a lot of time because I am very busy with my work.

However, I must be careful. Sometimes the size or color of the clothes is different from the picture on the screen. Also, I need to protect my personal information. I always use strong passwords. I think online shopping is very helpful for busy people around the world. It makes my life much better. I will buy a birthday gift for my mother next time.
