## テーマ: Shopping at the Supermarket (スーパーでの買い物)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Saturday. The weather is sunny. I go to the supermarket with my mother. The supermarket is very big. It has many foods and drinks.

First, we see the fruits. I see many apples. The apples are red and round. I like apples very much. We put four apples in our basket.

Next, we see the milk and eggs. The milk is white. The eggs are small and white. I drink milk every morning for breakfast. We buy one bottle of milk and ten eggs.

Then, we look for bread. The bread is near the door. It smells very good. My mother likes bread. We take one big bread.

We see some bananas. The bananas are yellow. They are long. They are very sweet. I like bananas too. We buy six bananas.

Now, we go to the register. A clerk is there. The clerk is a kind woman. She says "Hello." We say "Hello" to her.

My mother pays for the food. I have a big bag. I put the food in the bag. The bag is heavy now. I am very happy. We go home. Shopping is fun.
