## テーマ: A Visit to the Library (図書館へ行く)

### 英文パッセージ

I go to the library. The library is a big building. It has many books. The library is very quiet. Many people are in the library. They read books. 

I look for a book. I find a blue book. The blue book is about a cat. The cat is small. The cat is very cute. I like the cat. 

I sit on a chair. The chair is comfortable. I read the blue book. I am happy. 

My friend is there. Her name is Hana. Hana likes books. She has a green book. The green book is about stars. The stars are bright. 

We sit at a table. We read together. Hana is my good friend. 

The library has a big window. I see the park from the window. I see trees and flowers. The sun is yellow and bright. 

A woman is at the desk. She is a librarian. She is very kind. She helps me find books. 

I finish my book. I put the book on the shelf. I want to come to the library again. The library is a good place to study. I love the library.
