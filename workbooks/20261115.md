## テーマ: A Day at the Zoo (動物園での一日)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Sunday. The sun is shining, and the sky is blue. It is a beautiful day. I go to the zoo with my father and mother. We are very excited. We see many animals today.

First, we see a big elephant. The elephant is gray. It has a long nose and big ears. It is drinking water. It is very big and strong.

Next, we see the lions. There are three lions. They are sleeping under a big tree because it is hot. Lions are very strong animals. They have big teeth.

Then, we look at the giraffes. Giraffes are very tall. They have very long necks. They are eating green leaves from the tall trees. They are very quiet animals.

My favorite animal is the panda. The panda is black and white. It is sitting on the ground and eating bamboo. It looks very cute and friendly. I take many pictures of the panda with my camera.

At twelve o'clock, we have lunch at the zoo café. I eat a ham sandwich and drink cold orange juice. My father eats pizza. My mother eats a salad.

We are very happy. The zoo is a very fun place for everyone. We go home by bus at four o'clock. I want to go to the zoo again with my family.
