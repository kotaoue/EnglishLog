## テーマ: A Visit to the Farm (農場への訪問)

### 英文パッセージ

It is a beautiful morning. My name is Ken. Today, I visit my grandfather's farm. My mother and father come with me. The farm is in the countryside. The air is very clean.

Grandfather has many animals on his farm. First, we walk to the barn. I see big cows. The cows are black and white. They are very quiet. They eat green grass all day. Grandfather milks the cows every morning.

Next, we go to see the horses. There are three horses. One is brown, one is white, and one is black. I like the brown horse. Its name is Star. Star has long hair. I have a red apple in my hand. I give the apple to Star. Star is very hungry. He eats the apple quickly.

Near the house, there are many chickens. The chickens are small and yellow. They make a lot of noise. They walk around and look for food. I see ten chickens today.

At noon, we have a picnic. We sit on the grass under a big tree. The tree is old and tall. We eat ham sandwiches. We drink fresh milk from the farm. It is very delicious.

In the afternoon, we go to the garden. There are many vegetables and fruits. We pick red tomatoes and sweet apples. I put many apples in a big basket.

Now, it is evening. The sun goes down. I am tired but very happy. The farm is a wonderful place. I want to visit Grandfather again next month.
