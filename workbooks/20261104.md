## テーマ: A Rainy Day (雨の日)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is a rainy day. I look out the window. The sky is gray. I see many raindrops on the glass. I cannot go to the park today. I am at home with my cat. My cat's name is Tama. Tama is a small white cat. Tama likes to sleep on the soft sofa. I like to read books on rainy days. I have many books in my room.

I have a red umbrella. I have yellow boots. Sometimes I go to the store with my mother. I walk carefully. The ground is wet. I see a small puddle on the street. I jump into the puddle! It is very fun. My boots get dirty, but I am happy.

My mother makes hot tea for me. The tea is warm and sweet. I drink the tea in the kitchen. I like the smell of the rain. I like the sound of the rain. "Drip, drop, drip, drop." It is a very quiet day.

In the afternoon, I draw a picture. I use blue and green colors. I draw a big rainbow. A rainbow has many colors. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet. My mother looks at my picture. She says, "It is a beautiful rainbow!"

Rain is good for flowers. Rain is good for trees. The garden is very green today. The flowers are thirsty, so they drink the rain. I am happy at home. I love rainy days.
