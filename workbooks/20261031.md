## テーマ: Visiting the Library (図書館へ行く)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Saturday. The weather is sunny and warm. I go to the library near my house. The library is a very big building. It has many windows. Inside the library, it is very quiet. Many people are there. They read books and study. 

I see many bookshelves. The shelves have many books. I see small books and large books. I see red books and blue books. I look for a book to read. I like animals very much. I find a book about dogs and cats. The book is new. It has many pictures. The pictures are very beautiful. 

I take the book to a small table. I sit on a wooden chair. The chair is brown and comfortable. I read the book for one hour. Then, I see my friend, Yumi. She is reading a book about flowers. We smile at each other, but we do not talk. We must be quiet in the library because people are studying. 

I like the library very much. It is my favorite place in my town. I want to read many books every week. I go to the front desk. I show my library card to the librarian. I borrow three books. I put the books in my bag. I will read them at home with my family. Reading is very fun for me.
