## テーマ: The Benefits of Having a Pet (ペットを飼うことのメリット)

### 英文パッセージ

Many people around the world have pets. The most popular pets are dogs and cats. Some people also like birds, fish, or rabbits. Having a pet can change your life in many good ways.

First, pets help us feel less lonely. When you come home, a dog might wag its tail, or a cat might purr. This makes people feel happy and loved. For people who live alone, a pet is a great friend. They are always there to listen to you.

Second, pets help us stay active. For example, if you have a dog, you need to go for a walk every day. Walking is good for your heart and your body. It is also a good way to get some fresh air and see the sun. Even playing with a cat inside the house can be good exercise.

Third, pets can teach us about responsibility. You must feed them every day and give them water. You also need to clean their living space. This is very important for children to learn. They learn how to take care of another living thing.

However, having a pet is not always easy. Pets need a lot of time and money. You need to buy food and take them to a doctor when they are sick. But for many people, the love from a pet is more important than the cost. Pets are truly part of the family.
