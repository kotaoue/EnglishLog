## テーマ: Shopping at the Supermarket

### 英文パッセージ

I walk to the supermarket. Today is a sunny day. The sky is blue. The supermarket is near my house. It is a big store. I go there every Saturday morning. I need many things for my family.

I enter the store and take a shopping basket. First, I go to the fruit section. I see many fruits. I like apples, bananas, and oranges. The apples are red and sweet. The bananas are yellow and long. I put three apples and five bananas in my basket.

Next, I go to the milk and cheese area. It is a cold place. I buy two bottles of fresh milk. My children drink milk every morning for breakfast. I also buy some white eggs and yellow butter.

Then, I walk to the bread area. The smell of fresh bread is very good. I buy one big loaf of bread for sandwiches. After that, I look for rice, pasta, and meat. My family eats rice for dinner every day. Today, I buy some chicken too.

In the supermarket, there are many people. Some people are very busy and walk fast. Some people are slow and look at many things. I see my friend, Mary. We say "Hello!"

Finally, I go to the cashier. A kind woman works there. I put my food on the counter. She scans all my items. The total price is 4,000 yen. I pay with my money. I put all the food in my big bags. The bags are a little heavy. I walk back to my house. I am happy because I have good food. Cooking dinner for my family is very fun.
