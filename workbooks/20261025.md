## テーマ: A Picnic in the Park (公園でのピクニック)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Sunday. The weather is very nice. The sun is shining. My name is Ken. I go to the park with my family. My family is my father, my mother, and my sister. Her name is Hana. We have a big bag. In the bag, we have bread, apples, and water. We also have a ball.

We arrive at the park at ten o'clock. The park is very big and green. Many people are there. Some people are running. Some people are sitting on the grass. We find a good place under a big tree.

We sit down on a blue mat. We are hungry. My mother gives me a sandwich. It is very good. My sister eats a red apple. We talk and laugh. It is very fun. 

After lunch, I play with the ball with my sister. My father and mother watch us. Then, we see a small dog. The dog is cute. It wants to play, too. 

At three o'clock, we go home. I am a little tired, but I am very happy. I love the park. I want to go there again next Sunday. Spending time with family is important.
