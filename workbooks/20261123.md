## テーマ: Shopping at the Supermarket (スーパーでのお買い物)

### 英文パッセージ

My name is Ken. Today is a sunny Sunday. I go to the supermarket with my mother. The supermarket is big. It is near my house. We walk to the store together.

Inside the store, it is very cool. I see many colors. First, we go to the fruit section. I see red apples. I see yellow bananas. I see orange oranges. I like fruit very much. My mother puts five apples in a bag. I put three bananas in the basket. The bananas are yellow and long.

Next, we go to the milk section. I drink milk every morning. Milk is good for my body. We take one big bottle of milk. Then, we look for bread. My father likes bread for breakfast. We find some white bread. It is soft and nice.

Now, the basket is heavy. My mother carries the basket. We go to the cash register. There is a woman. She is a clerk. She scans our food. "Beep, beep, beep," goes the machine. My mother gives some money to the clerk. The clerk says, "Thank you."

We put the food in a big bag. We walk back to our house. I am a little tired, but I am happy. I want to eat a red apple. It is a very good day.
