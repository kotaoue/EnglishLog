## テーマ: The Role of Pets in Our Lives (私たちの生活におけるペットの役割)

### 英文パッセージ

Many people around the world enjoy living with pets. Cats and dogs are the most common choices, but some people prefer birds, fish, or even rabbits. Pets are not just animals; they are often treated as important members of the family.

One of the biggest benefits of having a pet is companionship. In today’s busy world, many people live alone or feel lonely. Having a pet waiting at home can make a big difference. For example, dogs are known for their loyalty. They are always happy to see their owners, which can improve a person's mood after a long day at work.

Additionally, pets can help reduce stress. Studies show that spending time with an animal can lower blood pressure and help people relax. Petting a cat or playing with a dog releases chemicals in the brain that make us feel happy. This is why some hospitals and schools use "therapy dogs" to help patients and students feel better.

Finally, pets encourage a healthy lifestyle. Dog owners, for instance, need to take their pets for walks every day. This ensures that the owners also get regular physical activity and fresh air. Taking care of a pet also teaches responsibility, especially for children. They learn that animals need food, water, and love to stay healthy.

In conclusion, pets bring many positive changes to our lives. They offer emotional support, improve our health, and teach us valuable life lessons.
