## テーマ: The Benefits of Walking Every Day (毎日歩くことのメリット)

### 英文パッセージ

Many people want to stay healthy, but they often feel they don't have enough time to go to the gym. However, there is a very simple and easy way to stay fit: walking. Walking is a basic exercise that almost everyone can do. You do not need expensive equipment or a gym membership. You only need a comfortable pair of shoes and a little time.

First, walking is very good for your heart. When you walk regularly, your heart becomes stronger and works better. This helps prevent many types of illnesses as you get older. Second, walking helps you feel mentally happy. When we walk outside in a park or near a river, we can see the trees, the sky, and feel the fresh air. This environment helps our minds relax. Many doctors say that walking reduces stress and helps people sleep better at night.

Third, walking is a great time to think or socialize. Some people like to walk alone to solve problems or find new ideas for work. Other people like to walk with their friends or family members. It is a perfect time to talk and enjoy spending time together without looking at smartphones.

If you want to start this habit, you should begin slowly. You do not have to walk for an hour every day. Just ten or twenty minutes a day is a good start. The most important thing is to continue doing it every day. Soon, you will feel more energetic, healthy, and happy.
