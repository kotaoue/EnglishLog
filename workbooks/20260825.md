## テーマ: The Benefits of Walking (ウォーキングのメリット)

### 英文パッセージ

Walking is one of the best ways to stay healthy. It is a simple exercise that almost everyone can do. You do not need expensive equipment or a gym membership to start. All you need is a good pair of shoes and some free time.

First, walking is very good for your physical health. When you walk, your heart beats faster, and your blood flows better. This helps prevent many diseases. Doctors recommend walking for at least thirty minutes every day. Even a short walk is better than no exercise at all.

Second, walking helps your mental health. Many people feel stressed because of work or study. When you walk outside, you can see trees, birds, and the sky. This helps you feel relaxed and happy. Some people find that walking helps them think of new ideas because their mind becomes clear.

Third, walking can be a social activity. You can walk with your friends, family, or even your dog. It is a great time to talk and share stories without distractions like television or smartphones.

Finally, walking helps you sleep better at night. If you move your body during the day, you will feel naturally tired when you go to bed. In conclusion, walking is easy, free, and very good for both your body and your mind. Why not start today?
