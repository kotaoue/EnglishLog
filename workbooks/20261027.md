## テーマ: Shopping at the Supermarket (スーパーでお買い物)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is a sunny Saturday. I go to a big supermarket with my mother. The supermarket is near my house. It is a very convenient place. We walk there slowly. Inside the supermarket, I see many colorful things. There are fruits, vegetables, meat, and drinks.

First, we go to the fruit area. I see many red apples and yellow bananas. My mother likes apples very much. She looks at the apples and takes five red apples. I like bananas because they are sweet. I take three yellow bananas. We put all the fruits in our shopping basket.

Next, we go to the milk area. My father drinks milk every morning for breakfast. We need two bottles of fresh white milk. We also look for bread. The bread is near the milk. It smells very good. We take one bag of brown bread.

Finally, we go to the cashier to pay. There are many people today, so we wait for five minutes. My mother pays with her money. The clerk is a very kind man. He smiles and says, "Have a nice day." We say "Thank you" and "Good bye." We carry the heavy shopping bags and walk back to our house. I am very happy because I like shopping with my mother.
