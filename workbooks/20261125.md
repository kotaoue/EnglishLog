## テーマ: A Birthday Party（お誕生日パーティー）

### 英文パッセージ

Today is Saturday. It is my birthday. I am ten years old. I am very happy today.

My friends come to my house. Their names are Ken and Hana. Ken has a big box. Hana has a small bag. The box and the bag are presents for me. I say, "Thank you!"

We are in the living room. There is a big table in the room. On the table, there is a chocolate cake. I like chocolate very much. The cake has ten candles. One, two, three... there are ten candles. The cake smells very good. 

There are many balloons in the room. We have red balloons, blue balloons, and yellow balloons. They are on the walls. My cat, Tama, is under the table. Tama looks at the balloons. 

My mother makes sandwiches and juice. The sandwiches have ham and cheese. The juice is orange juice. We eat the sandwiches and drink the juice. The sandwiches are very delicious.

Then, we sing a song together. We sing "Happy Birthday to You!" It is a fun song. I blow out the ten candles on the cake. My friends clap their hands. 

Now, I open my presents. Ken’s present is a blue ball. I like soccer, so I am happy. Hana’s present is a red book. It is a book about animals. I love dogs and cats. 

We go to the garden. The weather is very sunny. We play with the blue ball. We run and jump. We are very tired, but we are very happy. At five o'clock, my friends go home. I say, "Good-bye! See you!" It is a great birthday.
