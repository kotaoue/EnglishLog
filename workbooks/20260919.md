## テーマ: The Benefits of Reading Books (読書のメリット)

### 英文パッセージ

Reading is a wonderful hobby that many people enjoy all over the world. In the past, people only read paper books, but today, many people use e-readers or tablets. Regardless of how you read, books offer many important benefits for your brain and your daily life.

First, reading helps you gain new knowledge. When you read a book about history, science, or travel, you learn things that you did not know before. Even reading fiction, such as novels, can be helpful. It teaches you about different cultures and how people feel in different situations. This helps you understand others better and improves your communication skills.

Second, reading is good for your mental health. In our busy modern lives, we often feel stressed. Sitting down with a good book allows your mind to focus on one story and forget about daily problems. Many studies show that reading for just six minutes can reduce stress levels by more than 60%. It is like a quiet vacation for your brain.

Finally, reading is one of the best ways to improve your language skills. By reading many sentences, you can see how words are used in real contexts. For English learners, reading helps you remember new vocabulary and grammar naturally. You do not always need a dictionary; if you keep reading, you will often understand the meaning of a new word from the sentences around it.

Start reading something you like today. Even reading just ten pages a day can make a big difference in your life.
