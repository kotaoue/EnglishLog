## テーマ: Cooking Dinner with My Mother (お母さんと夕食作り)

### 英文パッセージ

Hello. My name is Ken. Today is Sunday. I am at home. I am in the kitchen with my mother. We are cooking dinner together. My mother likes cooking very much. I like eating her food.

We have many things on the kitchen table. We have three carrots, four potatoes, and two onions. We have some beef, too. My mother cuts the carrots and onions with a knife. She is very fast and careful. I help my mother. I wash the potatoes with water. The water is a little cold, but I like it. It is fun to cook.

We are making curry and rice today. Curry is my favorite food. It smells very good in the kitchen. My father is in the living room. He is watching a baseball game on TV. He is very hungry. My sister, Hana, is in her room. She is studying English for a test. She is hungry, too.

Now, the curry is ready. It is hot and looks great. I put four plates and spoons on the big table. My mother calls my father and sister. "Dinner is ready! Come to the kitchen," she says. We all sit down. We say, "It looks delicious." We eat the curry together. It is very yummy. I am very happy.
