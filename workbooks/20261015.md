## テーマ: The Importance of Drinking Water (水を飲むことの大切さ)

### 英文パッセージ

Water is very important for our bodies. Every person needs water to live. Most of our body is made of water. We should drink about eight glasses of water every day. This keeps us healthy and strong.

When we drink enough water, we feel very good. Our skin looks healthy and bright. Our brain works better, too. We can think more clearly and study hard. Drinking water helps us stay cool in the summer heat. It also helps our stomach digest food after we eat.

Many people like to drink coffee, tea, or soda. These drinks are okay sometimes, but water is the best choice. Water has no sugar and no calories. It is natural, clean, and cheap. It is the most important drink for everyone.

How can you drink more water every day? It is easy. First, always carry a small water bottle with you. Second, drink a large glass of water when you wake up in the morning. Third, drink water before you eat your breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

If you feel tired or have a headache, try drinking some water first. Sometimes our body is not tired; it is just thirsty. Water gives us energy to play and work. It is a very simple way to stay healthy. Let's drink more water every day for a long and happy life.
