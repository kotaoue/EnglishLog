## テーマ: Smartphones in Our Daily Life (日常生活の中のスマートフォン)

### 英文パッセージ

Many people around the world have a smartphone today. I have a smartphone, too. It is very small and light. I carry it in my pocket every day. I use my smartphone for many things.

First, I talk to my family and friends. We can talk anytime and anywhere. I also send messages. It is very fast and easy. I like talking to my mother on the phone.

Second, I take many photos with my smartphone. I take photos of flowers, food, and my cat. The photos are very clear and beautiful. I show the photos to my friends. We enjoy looking at them together.

Third, I use the internet on my smartphone. I search for information about many things. For example, I check the weather every morning. I also look for good restaurants. It is very helpful.

Finally, I listen to music on my smartphone. I like pop music. Music makes me feel happy and relaxed. I listen to it on the bus or at home.

My smartphone is very useful, but we must be careful. We should not use it for a very long time. We need to sleep well. Also, we should talk to people face to face. It is important to have a good balance. I like my smartphone, and I want to use it well.
