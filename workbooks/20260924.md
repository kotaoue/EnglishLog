## テーマ: The Rise of Remote Work (リモートワークの広がり)

### 英文パッセージ

In recent years, the way people work has changed a lot. Many people now work from their homes instead of going to an office. This is called "remote work." Technology makes this possible. With a computer and a good internet connection, people can do their jobs from almost anywhere.

Remote work has many benefits. First, people do not have to travel to the office. In big cities, commuting by train or bus takes a long time and can be very tiring. By working at home, people save time and energy. Second, workers can wear comfortable clothes and create their own workspace. They can also spend more time with their families.

However, there are also some difficulties. Some people feel lonely because they do not see their colleagues every day. Talking through a computer screen is different from talking in person. Also, it can be hard to separate work time and private time. Some people work too much because their office is always in their house.

To be successful at remote work, it is important to have a schedule. Taking regular breaks and having a quiet space to work can help a lot. Remote work is a new style of life for many people. It gives us more freedom, but it also requires good time management.
