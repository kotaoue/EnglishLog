## テーマ: The Joy of Travel (旅行の楽しさ)

### 英文パッセージ

I love traveling. Traveling is a wonderful hobby. Many people like to go to new places. I like to travel with my family. Sometimes, I travel with my friends. We go to different cities.

When I travel, I see many things. I see mountains, oceans, and tall buildings. Last summer, I went to Hokkaido. Hokkaido is a big island in Japan. The weather was very nice. It was cool and comfortable. I saw many colorful flowers. They were very beautiful. I took many pictures of the flowers.

Food is also important when I travel. In Hokkaido, I ate delicious seafood. I like sushi and crabs. The food was very fresh. It was great.

I also like to buy souvenirs. Souvenirs are small gifts for my friends. I buy keychains or cookies. My friends are happy when they get gifts.

Traveling makes me feel excited. I want to visit many countries in the future. I want to go to America and see the Statue of Liberty. I also want to go to France. I want to see the Eiffel Tower.

Travel is very good for us. We can learn about new cultures and meet new people. I want to plan my next trip soon. Where should I go next?
