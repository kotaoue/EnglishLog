## テーマ: Cooking at Home (家で料理をすること)

### 英文パッセージ

Cooking is a great hobby. Many people cook at home. I like to cook for my family. In the morning, I make breakfast. I eat eggs and toast. I drink orange juice, too. Breakfast is very important. It gives me energy for the day.

In the evening, I come home from work. Then, I start cooking dinner. Today, I want to make soup. I have carrots, potatoes, and chicken. I put them in a big pot with water. I wait for thirty minutes. The soup smells very good. My kitchen is warm and nice.

My mother is a good cook. She taught me how to cook when I was a child. She said, "Cooking is like magic." You mix things, and they become a delicious meal. I agree with her. When I cook, I feel relaxed.

I also like to go to the supermarket. I look at many kinds of food. I choose the best fish and meat. Sometimes I buy fruit. I like apples and bananas. Cooking at home is cheaper than eating at a restaurant. It is also very healthy. I love cooking every day.
