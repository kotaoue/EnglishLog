## テーマ: A Trip by Train (電車での旅行)

### 英文パッセージ

Hello. My name is Ken. I live in a big city. I love traveling by train. Last Sunday, I went on a trip to a small town.

I woke up at seven o'clock in the morning. I ate breakfast and left my house. I walked to the train station. The station was very busy. Many people were there. I went to the ticket machine and bought a ticket. The ticket was one thousand yen. 

I waited for my train on the platform. Soon, a green train arrived. I got on the train and sat by the window. I like the window seat because I can see the view. The train started to move. I saw many tall buildings first. Then, I saw green fields and a beautiful river. The weather was sunny, so the river was very pretty.

I felt hungry, so I ate an apple. It was sweet and delicious. I listened to music on my phone, too. After two hours, the train arrived at the small town. I got off the train. The air was fresh and the town was quiet. I was very happy to be there. I walked around and took many pictures with my camera. It was a great day.
