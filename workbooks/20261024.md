## テーマ: My Pet Dog (私のペットの犬)

### 英文パッセージ

I have a pet dog. His name is Max. Max is three years old. He is a very big dog. His fur is brown and white. He has a long tail and big, long ears. Max is very cute and I like him very much.

Every morning, Max and I go to the park near my house. We walk for thirty minutes. Max likes the park very much. There are many trees and green grass in the park. Max likes to run on the grass. He can run very fast. I throw a small yellow ball, and Max catches it. It is very fun for us.

Max is a friendly dog. He likes other dogs and people. When he sees a friend, he wags his tail. Many children in the park like to pet Max. He is very kind and never bites.

In the evening, Max sleeps in his bed. He is tired after playing all day. I give him good food and fresh water every day. Max is very happy when he eats his dinner.

I love Max very much. He is not just a pet. He is a member of my family. He makes me smile every day. I want to stay with Max for a long time. Having a dog is very wonderful for me.
