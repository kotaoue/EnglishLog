## テーマ: Eco-friendly Habits in Daily Life (日常生活におけるエコな習慣)

### 英文パッセージ

Today, many people are worried about the environment. Our Earth is facing serious problems like climate change and pollution. However, we can help protect nature by changing our daily habits. Small actions can make a big difference if everyone works together.

One of the easiest ways to be eco-friendly is to follow the "3 Rs": Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle. First, we should *reduce* the amount of trash we create. For example, when you go shopping, you can bring your own bag instead of using plastic bags from the store. This helps reduce plastic waste, which is very harmful to the ocean and animals.

Second, we should *reuse* things as much as possible. Instead of buying bottled water every day, you can use a reusable water bottle. This not only saves money but also reduces the number of plastic bottles that end up in the trash. Many people also donate their old clothes to others instead of throwing them away.

Finally, we should *recycle* paper, glass, and cans. Many cities have recycling programs that turn old items into new products. This saves energy and protects natural resources like trees.

In addition to the 3 Rs, we can save electricity at home. Remember to turn off the lights when you leave a room. You can also walk or ride a bicycle for short trips instead of driving a car. These habits are good for the Earth and also good for our health. Protecting the environment is not difficult. If we all start with small steps, we can keep our planet beautiful for the future.
