## テーマ: The Importance of Sleep (睡眠の大切さ)

### 英文パッセージ

Sleep is very important for all people. Every night, we go to bed and sleep. Why do we sleep? Our bodies need to rest. Our brains need to rest, too.

When we sleep, our bodies become strong. If you are a child, you grow while you sleep. For adults, sleep helps the body fix itself. Our brains are also very busy during sleep. The brain organizes information from the day. It helps us remember what we learned at school or work.

Most people need about seven to nine hours of sleep. If we do not get enough sleep, we feel very tired the next day. We cannot focus on our tasks. Sometimes we feel stressed or unhappy. It is hard to be kind to others when we are sleepy.

To sleep well, we can do a few simple things. First, make your bedroom dark and quiet. A comfortable bed is also helpful. Second, do not use a smartphone or watch TV before bed. The bright light tells your brain to stay awake. Third, try to go to bed at the same time every day. This helps your body find a good rhythm.

In the morning, a good sleep makes us feel fresh. We have a lot of energy to start the day. We can study well and play with friends. Good sleep is the best medicine for a healthy life. Let's value our sleep every night.
