## テーマ: A Fun Train Trip (楽しい電車の旅)

### 英文パッセージ

I really like traveling by train. Last Sunday, I went on a trip to a small town with my best friend, Ken. We met at Tokyo Station at nine o’clock in the morning. The station was very big and very busy. Many people were carrying big bags.

We bought our tickets at a machine. Then, we got on the train. The train was very clean and comfortable. We found our seats and sat by the window. I like looking outside the window. I saw many green trees, beautiful flowers, and small houses. The sky was blue and clear. It was a beautiful day.

On the train, we talked about many things. We talked about our hobbies. Ken likes playing soccer every Saturday. I like listening to music in my room. We also had lunch on the train. We ate some egg sandwiches and drank some cold orange juice. They were very delicious. Eating on a train is special.

After two hours, we finally arrived at the town. The town was very quiet and pretty. We walked from the station to a small park. There were many colorful birds and tall trees in the park. We took many pictures with our cameras to remember the trip.

In the evening, we went back home by train. I was a little tired at the end of the day, but I was very happy. Traveling by train is very fun and exciting. I want to go to a different place with Ken next time.
