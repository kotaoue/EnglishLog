## テーマ: Lunch Time at School (学校でのランチタイム)

### 英文パッセージ

It is lunch time. I am at school. I am in the classroom. I sit at my desk. I have a lunch box. My lunch box is blue. 

I open the box. I see a sandwich. The sandwich is big. I see an apple. The apple is red. I like apples. I have a bottle of water. I drink the water.

My friend, Tom, is here. He sits next to me. Tom has a sandwich, too. He has an orange juice. We eat lunch together. 

The teacher is in the room. Her name is Ms. White. She says, "Hello, everyone. Enjoy your lunch." We say, "Thank you, Ms. White."

The sandwich is good. The apple is sweet. We are happy. After lunch, we talk. We like school.
