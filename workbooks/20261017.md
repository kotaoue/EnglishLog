## テーマ: The Fun of Reading Books (本を読む楽しさ)

### 英文パッセージ

I like books very much. Reading is a great hobby. There are many kinds of books in the world. Some books are big, and some books are small. Some books have many pictures. These are good for children. Some books have only words. These tell long stories. I read books every day because I like them.

In the morning, I read the news on my tablet. It tells me about what is happening in the world today. In the afternoon, I like to read storybooks. Stories can take me to new places. I can see high mountains and blue oceans in my mind when I read. 

I also read books to learn new things. For example, I read books about cooking. Now, I can make a good breakfast. I also read books about animals like cats and dogs. Reading makes me very happy and smart.

I go to the local library once every week. The library is a big building with many books. I pick three or four books to borrow. I take them to my house. I sit on my favorite sofa and start to read. It is very quiet and peaceful.

Books are like good friends to me. They are always there when I am lonely. I love reading very much. Do you like books too? What is your favorite book? Reading opens a new world for everyone.
