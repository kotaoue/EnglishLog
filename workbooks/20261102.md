## テーマ: A Birthday Party (誕生日パーティー)

### 英文パッセージ

Today is a special day for me. It is my birthday. My name is Ken. I live in a small house with my father, my mother, and my sister. Today, my house is very busy. We are ready for my birthday party.

My friends arrive at two o'clock. My friend Tom comes first. He has a big blue box. It is a gift for me. Then, Hana and Yuki arrive. Hana gives me beautiful yellow flowers. Yuki gives me a nice birthday card. I say, "Thank you very much." I am very happy to see them.

We go to the dining room. There is a lot of food on the table. We see pizza, chicken, and salad. My mother is a good cook. She makes a big cake for me. The cake is chocolate. It has ten candles on it. We sing the "Happy Birthday" song together. I blow out the candles. The cake is very sweet and delicious. We drink orange juice and tea.

After we eat, we go to the garden. The weather is sunny and warm. We play with a ball. We run and jump. We laugh a lot. It is very fun.

My father has a camera. He says, "Everyone, look here!" He takes a photo of us. In the photo, everyone has a big smile.

I love my birthday. I like spending time with my family and friends. It is a wonderful day. I want to say thank you to everyone.
