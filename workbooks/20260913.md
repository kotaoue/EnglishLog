## テーマ: The Importance of Good Sleep (質の良い睡眠の重要性)

### 英文パッセージ

Sleep is a very important part of our daily lives. Many people think sleep is just a time for the body to rest, but it is much more than that. When we are asleep, our bodies and brains are actually very busy doing many important jobs to keep us healthy.

First, sleep helps our brain function better. After a good night's sleep, it is much easier to remember new things and make good decisions. On the other hand, if we do not sleep enough, we might feel very tired and have trouble focusing at school or work. We may also become grumpy or feel sad more easily because our emotions are not stable.

Second, sleep is essential for our physical health. While we sleep, our bodies work to fix tired muscles and help us grow. It also strengthens our immune system, which helps us fight off colds and other illnesses. Most adults need about seven to nine hours of sleep every night to feel their best. Children and teenagers need even more sleep because their bodies and brains are still developing quickly.

To improve your sleep quality, it is helpful to have a regular routine. You should try to go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, even on weekends. Also, it is better to avoid using smartphones or computers right before sleep. The blue light from these screens can tell your brain to stay awake. Instead, try relaxing activities like reading a printed book or taking a warm bath.
