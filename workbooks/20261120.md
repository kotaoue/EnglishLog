## テーマ: My Morning Routine（私の朝のルーティン）

### 英文パッセージ

I wake up at seven o'clock. I am happy. I see the sun. I get out of bed. I go to the bathroom. I wash my face. The water is cold. I like it. I brush my teeth. My toothbrush is blue. 

The sun is yellow and bright. The birds sing in the trees. I can hear them from my window. My room is small but clean. I have a desk and a chair. There is a picture of my family on the wall. 

Then, I go to the kitchen. My mother and father are there. My mother says, "Good morning!" I say, "Good morning, Mom." My father reads a newspaper. He drinks coffee. The coffee smells good. I sit at the table. I eat breakfast. I eat bread and an egg. I drink orange juice. It is sweet. My dog, Pochi, is under the table. Pochi wants some bread. I give a small piece of bread to Pochi. Pochi is happy.

After breakfast, I put on my clothes. I wear a white shirt and blue pants. I look at the clock. It is eight o'clock. I take my bag. My bag is heavy. I have books in my bag. 

I go to the door. I put on my shoes. I say, "Goodbye!" to my family. The weather is very nice today. There are no clouds in the sky. I walk to school. I see my friend, Ken. Ken has a big smile. We walk to school together. We talk about games. School is fun. I like my morning.
